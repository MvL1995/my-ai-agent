Privacy Notice

Data controller: ProofFirst Studio.

We collect the name, email, company, message and privacy consent that you provide. We also read UTM parameters from the URL automatically and add a fixed workflow identifier.

We use this data only to assess your enquiry, reply and measure enquiry sources. We do not sell your data or make automated decisions about you.

Vercel hosts this site. Formspree processes the form and sends email notifications. These providers may process data outside Malaysia. Formspree submission history is retained for 30 days; notification emails are deleted when no longer needed.

Use the independent privacy request form below to request access, correction or deletion, or withdraw consent. We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 calendar days. You may choose not to submit the main enquiry form.
