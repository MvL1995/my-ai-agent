Critical illness cover ≠ Medical Card

They address different needs. Understanding the difference helps you see what may be missing.

Medical Card

Main purpose
Hospital and eligible medical expenses
Common uses
Hospitalisation, surgery and treatment
How it works
Eligible expenses are handled according to plan limits and policy terms

Critical illness cover

Main purpose
A cash payment when the policy definition and conditions are met
Common uses
Living costs, mortgage payments, interrupted income and family commitments
How it works
Claims are assessed against the illness definitions and conditions in the policy

Coverage, definitions, waiting periods, exclusions and claim conditions vary by plan. Refer to the official policy terms for the actual details.
